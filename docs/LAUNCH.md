# Launch checklist and communications

- [ ] Clear SentinelX brand and SNTX symbol; existing name collisions found.
- [ ] Owner-approved legal review, terms, privacy and incident contacts.
- [ ] Independent contract audit; fuzz, invariant and malicious-token suites.
- [ ] Owner-controlled multisig and reviewed timelock deployments.
- [ ] Funded testnet deployment, explorer verification and real claim transaction.
- [ ] Persistent authenticated profiles, validation roles, signed submissions and database RLS tested.
- [ ] Canonical oracle/RWA integrations independently verified.
- [ ] Persistent worker deployed, checkpoint backup and restore tested, external heartbeat alerting enabled.
- [ ] Dedicated redundant RPC, rate limits, historical coverage and deep-reorg recovery exercised.
- [ ] Admin audit records anchored externally and dispute process operational.
- [ ] Explicit owner authorization before mainnet issuance, sales, liquidity or public rewards.

## Revenue policy proposal
Model actual collected revenue as 50% operations, 25% independently verified contributor rewards, 15% security reserve, 10% community development. These are configurable assumptions; no revenue or reward commitment exists. Public reward epochs must publish funding receipts, eligibility, fixed-point scores, Merkle allocation files, liabilities, claims and dispute outcomes before activation.
